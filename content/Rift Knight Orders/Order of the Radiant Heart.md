## Feature: 

You can stabilize any dying creature as a bonus action without a Medicine check. Once per long rest, you may use a **Rally of the Heart** — when an ally within 30 ft drops to 0 HP, they instead fall to 1 HP and gain temporary hit points equal to your proficiency bonus. When you do so, faint golden light glows from your palms or eyes.


# History


## Origins

**Support Division.**  
Formed after the disastrous **Battle of the Shattered Plains**, where thousands of Rift Knights died for lack of medical support. The Union decreed a new Order devoted to restoring, reinforcing, and protecting the warriors of the Rift.

## Achievements

**The Salvation at Emberfast**: Evacuated and healed over 3,000 soldiers during the Purifier Crusade, despite being declared “noncombatants.”

**The Shroudlift Miracle**: Restored a Rift-blighted city to livable conditions through mass purification rituals.

**Operation Veilguard**: Maintained a defensive ward for thirty days against demonic siege without reinforcement.

## Defeats

**The Red Triage**: Chose to heal enemy prisoners over Union officers — leading to a deadly breakout.

**The Hollow Oath**: A schismatic sect tried to “heal” demons by removing their souls, resulting in hundreds of twisted abominations.


# Culture

## Philosophy

The Radiant Heart believes preservation is the purest form of warfare. Every soldier saved, every limb restored, every ounce of courage rekindled - these are victories as sacred as any slain demon.

To them, life itself is an act of defiance against the Rift’s endless hunger. While the flame could be a gift from the gods, they see the body as the greatest divine gift. When the battlefield burns, they walk through the fire to heal the fallen - for even a broken knight can rise again to fight another day.

## Faith / Belief



## Demeanor

- Calm, patient, compassionate even amidst horror

- Believe every life is sacred, but not _equal_ - resources go to those who can still serve

- In battle, serene and unflinching; they heal under fire without hesitation



## Order Flaw

The Medics often refuse to prioritize, refusing to choose who lives and who dies. Their hesitation or idealism can cost entire squads. Some say they “waste miracles” on the undeserving.

## Combat Doctrine

**Support / Restoration / Defense.**  
Their warriors wield shields, radiant wards, and restorative magic. The Mending Heart specializes in prolonged engagements, bolstering allied morale and endurance while gradually overwhelming the enemy with attrition and faith.


# Organization

## Head Fortress

**Home:** The Sanctum of Respite — a vast cathedral-hospital built atop the cliffs of the Celindor Vale. Its halls glow with warm light, and the air smells faintly of incense and ozone. Wounded soldiers from across the Union are brought here.

**Rule:** _Stewardship._  
The Order governs gently, acting as caretakers and protectors of the Vale and its people. They heal without judgment — though they reserve their best miracles for Union forces.


## Mobility

They travel in mobile sanctuaries known as **Heartwains** — reinforced wagons of ivory and bronze, filled with apothecaries, divine wards, and automated healing constructs. Each Heartwain can establish a field hospital in under an hour.

## Training

Initiates undergo the **Trial of the Shattered Body** — a ritual in which they are gravely wounded and must heal themselves without aid, guided only by divine focus and willpower.

They study **anatomy, battlefield triage, divine warding, pain endurance**, and **psychological stabilization**. Advanced members train to project their auras through hymns, chants, and battle prayers that empower allies.

## Restricted

They do **not train Barbarians or Wizards.** Their discipline demands faith, restraint, and compassion — qualities they deem incompatible with rage or intellectual pursuits.

**Normal.** The Order maintains consistent ranks and training, but turnover is high due to the emotional toll and danger of their missions.

## Reputation

**Consistently Effective.**  
Rarely celebrated, but almost always vital. Their quiet efficiency and unbreakable faith make them the backbone of many successful campaigns.

## Members

**Saint-Calix Vorn**, known as the _Unbroken Surgeon_. During the Siege of Brimhold, he single-handedly held a triage line for three days, amputating, healing, and reanimating fallen comrades with divine light. When he finally collapsed, his heart still beat — separated from his body, pulsing with radiant energy. It became the Order’s holiest relic: _The Radiant Heart._
