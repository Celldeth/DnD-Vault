## The Order of the Iron Rain - "Rainmakers"

### Philosophy

> _“From the barrel, falls judgment.”_  
> The Iron Rain believes that divine will can be expressed through the cold precision of steel and gunfire. They see warfare as a sacred rite, cleansing the world of corruption and chaos. Precision, coordination, and faith in the Flame of Endurance are their pillars.



### Symbol & Motto

**Symbol:** A downward-pointing thunderbolt formed of three intersecting gun barrels, encircled by a burning ring.
   
**Colors:** Gunmetal grey, ash black, and molten orange accents.
   
**Motto:** _“Let it rain.”_ — A phrase both prayer and battle cry, invoked before unleashing volleys of gunfire or bombardment.




### Why Was The Order Founded?

**Counter-Offensive:**  
Created during the aberrant invasion when Slaads began assaulting Union forward bases. The Iron Rain were one of the first orders to use firearms and used them to take the fight back to the hellion rift, capable of banishing horrors at range.




### **Demeanor**

**Weapons over magic.**  
The Iron Rain distrust raw sorcery; magic is viewed as volatile and corruptible. Instead, they favor _bound magic_ — spellwork sealed into weapons, ammunition, or machinery.  
Their way is the art of precision, volume, and discipline. If the mission calls for magic, it will be delivered from the barrel of a gun.



### **Order Flaw**

**They announce themselves before every battle. Loudly. With banners. And fireworks.**  
Their doctrine demands that the enemy know who brings the storm. The Iron Rain deploys flares, vox-horns, and roaring bombardments before engagements — a show of dominance that inspires allies and terrifies mortals, but makes stealth nearly impossible.  
To them, surprise is dishonorable; a storm never hides.


### **A Figure of Legend**

**High Marksman Ser Ephram Kael**, who, during the Siege of Durn Hollow, single-handedly defended the last bastion by maintaining an unbroken volley for twelve hours. He claimed the Flame guided his aim—and that he never missed a single shot.  
His rifle, **“Voice of the Tempest,”** is passed down to exceptional veterans.

**Ser Rhazian Hailbrand**, the “Tempest Knight.”  
In the Battle of Sere’s Fall, Rhazian ordered an orbital bombardment from their floating fortress while still on the ground — calling down the storm directly upon his position. His sacrifice annihilated three demonic warhosts and closed a breach into the Abyss. His ashes are kept in the bolt chamber of every Iron Rain heavy weapon, to remind the bearer what it costs to _be the storm._

### **Home Town**

**Hollowmarch Bastion** — a fortified settlement built into a jagged escarpment at the border of Sunspire’s western territories.

- Terrain: rocky ridges, intermittent rainfall, long fields of hardy brush and stony plateaus.
    
- The constant echo of thunder from storms (natural and artillery) defines the area.
    



### **Rule Over Hometown**

- **Stewardship.**  
    The Order oversees Hollowmarch as guardians rather than rulers. Civilians live freely, but the Order enforces martial discipline and maintains massive foundries for gunmetal and powder production.
    



### **Does the Order Constantly Move?**

**Mobile Fortress: “The Thunderhead Citadel.”**  
A skyborne citadel — half ship, half temple — that drifts along the Rift’s edge. It rains fire from above, supported by flotillas of smaller gunships and drop-barges. Wherever the fortress passes, the world trembles beneath its bombardment.



### **Combat Doctrine**

**Ranged / Siege / Shock and Awe.**  
The Iron Rain win wars through saturation fire, long-range artillery, and aerial strikes. They deploy small squads to designate targets, followed by overwhelming bombardment. Once the world burns, survivors are cleansed by close-range gunfire or steel.


### **Order’s Unique Training**

**Drilled in trench warfare, demolition, and attrition tactics from early stages.**  
Every initiate must endure a year-long rotation under live bombardment, learning to fight, rebuild, and fight again while under endless fire.  

Their training blends meditative gun drills with chants of precision and timing; each volley must strike like the beat of a single war drum. Pain, fatigue, and even fear are trained out — only focus remains.



### **Classes Not Trained**

**Druids and Monks** rarely find place here.  
The Iron Rain’s mechanized and militarized existence leaves no room for naturalist philosophy or unarmed tradition. They respect nature only as something to be burned into sterile order.



### **Special Equipment**

**Modified Weapons & Magic Charms.**  
Each Iron Rain Knight bears a _Stormforged Armament_ — weapons inscribed with storm-sigils that channel elemental energy (lightning, fire, thunder).  
Senior Knights may bear _Raincaller Flares_ — portable targeting totems that can summon orbital strikes or call the Thunderhead’s guns.  



### **Faith / Beliefs**




### **Strength**

**Normal — Fit for battle.**  
The Iron Rain suffered severe losses during the Siege of Eltara’s Spine but rebuilt through efficient recruitment and rapid production. They remain stable, though attrition is constant.




### **Allies**

**Friendly With:** _The Forge Guild_ (who maintain their weapons and skycraft).  
Mutual respect between craftsmen of war.  
They also maintain a pragmatic alliance with _The Bright Guard_, who serve as forward scouts to mark artillery coordinates.



### **Enemies**

- **Aberrations & Riftspawn.**  
    Especially the horrors of the _Fathomless Maw_, whose void-skinned monstrosities nullify gunfire unless bullets are sanctified by the Flame.
    



### **Order Effectiveness**

**Battlefield prowess and honor widely recognized.**  
The Iron Rain are one of the most feared and respected Orders within the Union. Their campaigns leave nothing standing — for better or worse.  
Civilians often dread their “liberation,” knowing it means scorched earth.


### **Major Achievements**

- **The Cleansing of Hollowmarch Rift:** Closed a rift breach by bombarding it continuously for three days, collapsing the surrounding terrain into molten slag.
    
- **The Silver Barrage:** Coordinated 500 riflemen to eliminate a demon army of 10,000 from miles away under blinding rain.
	
- **The Glassing of the Cindervale:** Turned an entire Rift breach into molten glass, halting a demonic incursion that threatened three cities.
    
- **The Retaking of Bastion Aurum:** Established artillery positions that saved the Bright Guard from annihilation.
    
- **The Skyfire Accord:** Developed the first cross-order orbital relay system — enabling Orders to coordinate fire support for the first time.



### **Shameful Defeats**

**The Siege of Eltara’s Spine:** Overconfidence led them to bombard friendly positions, annihilating allied infantry before the demons arrived.  
To this day, other Orders refer to “Iron Rain” as both a name and a curse — “When it rains, it kills everyone.”
