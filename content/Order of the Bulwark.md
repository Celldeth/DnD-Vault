# History


## Origins

**Strategic Defensive Division.**  
Born from the ashes of **The Blackwall Collapse**, when demonic hordes breached the Union’s primary northern bulwark. Survivors of that catastrophe swore an oath — that no line would fall again so long as a Fang still drew breath.

## Achievements

Siege of Hollow Deep: Held an impossible defense that inspired a Union-wide resurgence.

The Wall of Souls: Built a living bulwark of warriors who withstood demonic tide for 12 days.

The Blackgate Accord: Personally oversaw the reinforcement of five major rift fronts in a single year.


## Defeats

The Fall of Bastion 9: Refused orders to withdraw, resulting in the annihilation of 700 Knights and the capture of vital relics.

The Iron March: Their stubborn defense allowed enemy flanking forces to cut through unprotected terrain, dooming thousands.



# Culture



## Philosophy

Gatekeepers believe that to survive is to endure. To remain longer than the enemy is to win. They embody the ideal of the immovable warrior — an unrelenting wall of iron and willpower that refuses to fall no matter the odds.

To them, defense is not passive - it is an act of domination. Every deflected blow, every inch denied to the enemy, is a declaration that the Rift shall not consume this world. Their creed: _Hold, and the world holds with you._

## Faith / Belief



## Demeanor

Stoic and disciplined; emotion is weakness in their eyes.

Speak little, act decisively.

Have no patience for cowards or strategists who retreat.

Treat every fortress, trench, or chokepoint as a test from the gods.


## Order Flaw

Their pride in defense borders on obsession. The Bulwark will refuse retreat, even when tactical withdrawal would save countless lives. Their stubbornness often leads to Pyrrhic victories or glorious last stands that leave no survivors.


## Combat Doctrine

**Melee and Defensive Warfare.**
Experts in shield formations, fortress sieges, and chokepoint combat. Their squads often operate as defensive anchors, creating strongholds in the midst of chaos for others to rally behind.

When they attack, it’s with disciplined, grinding force - a hammer of unyielding steel rather than reckless fury.


# Organization

## Head Fortress

**Home:** *Shieldwall Keep*, a fortress carved into a mountain’s side at the Rift’s edge. Its outer walls are engraved with the names of every fallen member.  

**Rule:** _Direct stewardship._ The Shieldwall houses thousands of refugees under their rigid, martial governance. All citizens must train, serve, or build.



## Mobility

They travel in Siege Trains - massive, armor-plated crawlers powered by bound earth elementals. Each train carries a garrison’s worth of troops, repair forges, and mobile bulwarks that can deploy into a fortress within hours.

## Training

Training is called The Crucible of Stone. Recruits spend one month buried waist-deep in sand, holding their shield and weapon against the elements — eating, sleeping, and fighting from that position.

The Order teaches measured breathing, pain suppression, counter-strike tactics, and unbreakable formation drills. Many members undergo Rite of the Iron Spine, where a metal graft reinforces their backbone, symbolizing their vow never to bow.

## Restricted Classes

They do not train Rogues or Sorcerers. The Fang disdains improvisation and unstructured power; every blow and spell must serve the formation’s unity.
