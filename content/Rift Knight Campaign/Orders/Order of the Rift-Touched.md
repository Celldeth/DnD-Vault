_(Commonly called Seers)_

Symbol: A spiraling eye framed by twisted chains.

Colors: Deep violet, burnt orange, and black.

Motto: “Master the warp.”



# Player Background: *Seer*

**Skill Proficiencies:** Arcana, Investigation  
**Tool Proficiencies:** Alchemist’s Supplies or Tinker’s Tools  
**Languages:** Abyssal  
**Equipment:**
- A piece of Riftstone sealed in glass (glows faintly in your presence)
- A small journal filled with warped diagrams and sigils
- Common clothes reinforced with Rift-thread stitching
- 10 gp in strange, shimmering currency
- A weapon etched with a single Rift rune


## Feature: Warp-Bound

You can sense the presence of demonic corruption within 30 feet as a faint vibration in your mind. Additionally, when interacting with other Orders or Union organizations, your presence carries a mix of fear and unknown - granting advantage on Charisma (Intimidation) checks against them.



# History


## Origins

Purpose: Strategic Defensive & Counter-Offensive.
They were formed after a catastrophic incursion nearly destroyed a frontier stronghold. Survivors used scavenged warped weapons and items to turn the tide - leading to the Order’s founding, dedicated to mastering that which once nearly consumed them.

## Achievements

Contained "The Rupture", preventing a merge of planar and material space.

Created the Warp Bastion, a mobile fortress that can phase partially out of reality.

Perfected the use of “tether sigils” to bind demons as living weapons.

## Defeats

The Silent Spiral Incident: An experiment to merge demonic and mortal minds resulted in mass insanity. Entire division executed to prevent contagion.

The Purity Rebellion: Attempted cleansing by other Orders led to civil infighting; their leader was killed by a supposed ally.



# Culture



## Philosophy

The Order believes salvation lies not in resisting corruption, but controlling it. They wield demonic infused relics to control enemy forces, bend space, and manipulate the battlefield. Their members see themselves as necessary evil - they tarnish their souls, so others don't have too.

## Faith / Belief



## Demeanor

Planners, knowledge is power, use dead bodies for experimentation.
Coldly analytical, eerily calm, and fascinated by the unnatural.

## Order Flaw

Practices are weird and unsavory.
Rumors persist of rituals involving demonic ichor injections, whispered bargains, and soldiers “reborn” with warped flesh. Other Orders distrust them, calling them demon lovers.

## Combat Doctrine

Magic + Ranged + Control.
They specialize in battlefield manipulation - magic barriers, illusionary fields, trap glyphs. They rarely engage head-on, preferring to control tempo and space.


# Organization

## Head Fortress

Their home fortress is Bastion Nadir, built into a crater where a demon dwelling once stood. The terrain is cracked obsidian earth and glowing veins of (enter stone).

### Rule

Distant stewardship.
The Order provides defense but little governance. Locals see them as both protectors and overseers, more warden than ruler.


## Mobility

Semi-mobile fortress: The Twisted Bastion.
A shifting citadel of fused stone and metal that travels by teleporting powered by bound ethereal energy. The process takes an immense amount of power, requiring a ritual of hundreds of casters and sacrificing demonic infused relics - limiting use. It leaves scars of corrupted earth wherever it moves from.

## Training

Drilled in ethereal infiltration, demonic warfare, and survival tactics.

Pain tolerance and arcane focus are foundational.

Indoctrination rituals use demonic ichor in controlled amounts to warp the flesh, granting each knight varying demonic boons.

Meditation under demonic exposure is common; trainees must practice to keep the corruption at bay, reciting mantras - reminding themselves of their duty.


## Equipment

Warp-Touched Armor: Grants short-distance blink (misty step once per long rest).

Twistcore Relic: A pulsating Rift shard embedded in a focus device, used to channel battlefield control effects.

Modified Weapons: Many wield chained glaives or rifles with Rift-etched runes that bend trajectory or create small explosions of spatial distortion.

## Order Strength

They follow the Doctrine of the Controlled Rift — belief that the Rift is not evil, only misunderstood. Demons are seen as corrupted reflections of potential power. Their faith lies in Balance through mastery.

## Recruitment

Below average — recovering from a near-purge.
The Twisted Warp was once declared rogue by the High Council after a failed containment that killed thousands. They were nearly annihilated, surviving now only as a remnant force tolerated for their rare expertise.

## Alliances

- The Arcanum (mutual arcane respect)
- The Steward Society (strategic toleration)
- The Order of the Hound, 


## Enemies / Rivals

- Demons, especially entities of chaos.
- The Bright Guard, who view them as blasphemous abominations.
- The Order of the Iron Rain, whose technological purity clashes with their “tainted” science.

Paladins and Clerics, their use of demonic corruption is seen as heretical.

Druids, their methods desecrate nature.

## Reputation

Secret – campaigns go unremembered.
Their missions are classified, their victories unspoken. Officially, they don’t exist. Yet strange lights are sometimes seen across battlefields before enemy lines collapse into themselves.
