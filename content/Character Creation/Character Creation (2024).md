
### Choosing a Class:

You can choose any class or subclass from the [[content/Character Creation/Classes/Classes (2024)]] page

### Choosing Origins:

A character's origin includes two elements: [[content/Character Creation/Backgrounds (2024)]] and [[content/Character Creation/Species (2024)]]. 


#### Think about your backstory

- Who raised you?
- Who was your dearest childhood friend?
- Did you grow up with a pet?
- Have you fallen in love? If so, with whom?
- Did you join an organization, such as a guild or religion? If so, are you still a member of it?
- What elements of your past inspire you to go on adventures now?



### Determining your ability score:

-   All ability scores start at 8.

-   Distribute 27 ability points with no score higher than 16.

-   Apply your background bonus.


### Finishing Details

#### Describe Appearance and Personality

###### Ideas based on ability scores

| Ability Score | High Score Description                     | Low Score Description                     |
| ------------- | ------------------------------------------ | ----------------------------------------- |
| Strength      | Muscular, Sinewy, Protective, Direct       | Weak, Slight, Self-conscious, Indirect    |
| Dexterity     | Lithe, Dynamic, Fidgety, Poised            | Jittery, Clumsy, Hesitant, Unsteady       |
| Constitution  | Energetic, Hale, Hearty, Stable            | Frail, Squeamish, Lethargic, Fragile      |
| Intelligence  | Decisive, Logical, Informative, Curious    | Artless, Illogical, Uninformed, Frivolous |
| Wisdom        | Serene, Considerate, Attentive, Wary       | Rash, Distracted, Oblivious, Naive        |
| Charisma      | Charming, Commanding, Hilarious, Inspiring | Pedantic, Humorless, Reserved, Tactless   |

- What person or people do you care most about?
- What's your deepest fear?
- On your adventures, will you seek knowledge, wealth, glory, enlightenment, justice, mercy, power, or something else?


#### Choose an Alignment:

- One order (lawful, chaotic, neutral)
- One morality (good, evil, or neutral)

**Examples**:

| Alignment | Description                                                                                                                                        |
| --------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Lawful    | act in accordance with law, tradition, or personal codes.                                                                                          |
| Chaotic   | follow their whims, valuing personal freedom above all else.                                                                                       |
| Neutral   | those who prefer to avoid moral questions and don't take sides, doing what seems best at the time.                                                 |
| Good      | endeavoring to do the right thing as either dictated by society (lawful) or themselves (chaotic).                                                  |
| Evil      | acting to take or harm those around them, either within the limits of their order (lawful) or within their personal hatred and bloodlust (chaotic) |
