
# Choosing a Race:

You can choose any race from the [[content/Character Creation/Races\|Races]] page

## Languages

Your character can speak, read, and write Common and other languages listed by your selected race. You may confer with your DM to replace languages that would be appropriate for your character. The Player's Handbook offers a list of languages to choose from.

# Choosing a Class:

You can choose any class or subclass from the [[content/Character Creation/Classes/Classes]] page

## Equipment

Defer to your character class and select equipment based on the options there.

# Choosing a Background:

[[content/Character Creation/Backgrounds/Backgrounds]] are all available. Custom backgrounds need to be approved.

# Determining your ability score:

-   All ability scores start at 8.
    
-   Distribute 27 ability points with no score higher than 16.
    
-   Apply your racial bonus.
