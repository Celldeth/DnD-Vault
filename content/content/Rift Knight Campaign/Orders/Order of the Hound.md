### **Feature — Creature of the Rift**

You can sense the taint of corruption. As long as you spend at least one minute focusing, you can detect whether a demon or a creature warped by demon ichor has passed through an area within the last 24 hours, and the approximate direction of travel.  

Additionally, beasts and monstrosities are less hostile to you unless provoked.



## **Philosophy**

The Order of the Hound believes survival itself is sacred — not as endurance, but as domination over the predators of the world.  
They hunt the abyss as one might hunt a beast, tracking demonic lords across the Rift’s endless wastelands. They revere _instinct, obedience, and the pack bond_ — the belief that no knight fights alone, and that loyalty is greater than faith.  
To the Hound, mercy is disease; hesitation is betrayal. They hunt until there is _nothing left to flee._



## **Symbol, Colors, & Motto**

- **Symbol:** A snarling wolf’s skull impaled on a hunting spear, its jaw chained shut.
    
- **Colors:** Bone white, blood red, and dark grey leathers.
    
- **Motto:** _“Cry once. Strike twice.”_ — referencing their tradition of issuing a single, bone-deep howl before an assault.
    



## **Why Was the Order Founded?**

- **Purpose:** _Hunters and Executioners._  
    After the demons made dominion outside the rift, many demons spread corruption into surviving forests, ruins, and villages. The Union founded the Order of the Hound to _track, contain, and exterminate_ these roaming horrors when large-scale bombardment was impossible or too costly.  
    
    Their formation was the first true _counter-insurgency force_ of the Union.
    



## **Demeanor**

**Hunters/Trackers.**  
  The Hounds are primal and instinct-driven. They operate in small, silent packs, moving through smoke and ruin with brutal precision. They scorn rigid tactics and bureaucracy.
  
  They are loyal to their commanders, but only truly obey the squad leader they respect. Discipline is enforced by fang and knife, not law.
  



## **Order Flaw**

**Practices are unsavory, and they know it.**  
The Hounds are rumored to use demonic ichor to track their prey. Some say they drink from their kills to “taste” corruption’s trail. They deny nothing, but never confirm.  

Other Orders call them _feral_, _half-cursed,_ or _too close to the abyss._ The Hounds simply reply: “To hunt the beast, we must run beside it.”
   



## **A Figure of Legend**

**Ors Telvar, the Huntsman.**  
During the Night of Shattered Chains, Telvar tracked a greater demon across seventy leagues of black fog. When his pack fell, he continued alone, wounded and half-blind, and returned seven days later carrying the fiend’s severed head.  

He never spoke again. his throat scarred from the final howl that broke the demon’s will.



## **Home Town & Terrain**

**Hearthden**, a sprawling den-city built into a petrified forest at the Rift’s edge. Massive fossilized trunks form watchtowers and nesting grounds for their beasts. The air is damp, filled with spores and ghostlight.  
Hearthden smells of gunpowder, blood, and the musk of animals — a city that breathes.



## **Rule Over Hometown**

**Distant Rule.**  
The Hounds view Hearthden as a safe haven, not a kingdom. The civilians who live there are “tame folk” — tolerated but not respected. The Order rarely returns except to rest, rearm, or release the wounded to die in peace.




## **Mobility**

**Chain of War Wagons (“The Huntline”).**  
Each pack travels with a string of rune-etched wagons, pulled by armored beasts or arcane engines.  
The wagons are half barracks, half butchery — filled with weapon racks, meat hooks, tracking altars, and the constant growl of hounds locked in iron cages.




## **Combat Doctrine**

**Stealth / Close Quarters / Hunters.**  
The Hounds excel in tracking and ambush warfare. Their doctrine centers on _stalking, striking, and isolating_ demonic prey.  
While they lack the overwhelming firepower of the Iron Rain, they compensate with surgical brutality — and the uncanny ability to find what others cannot.




## **Order’s Unique Training**

**Training includes surviving and dominating native fauna, producing ruthless hunters.**  
Initiates are taken into the Rift and abandoned for a lunar cycle with nothing but a knife and their instincts. Those who return have learned to _hunt, tame, or become the hunted._  
Later training hones them into synchronized packs — coordinated through howls, whistles, and psychic bonds formed through ritual bloodletting.




## **Classes Unavailable**

**Artificers and Wizards.**  
The Hounds disdain complex arcana. They trust the senses, not schematics. To them, thinking too much dulls the kill.  
Sorcery born of instinct (Sorcerers, Warlocks, Rangers, Barbarians) is respected — as long as it serves the Hunt.




## **Special Equipment**

**Beast Companions & Magic Tattoos.**  
Every Hound bonds with a hell-hound - a creature from the planes of Acheron, Gehenna, and the Nine Hells. They are intelligent, relentless, and terrifying.  
Veterans bear _Howl Marks_, tattoos that amplify their senses and allow short bursts of preternatural speed or feral strength when invoked.

## **Order Strength**

**Below Average.**  
The Hounds were nearly wiped out during the “Rift Winter,” when half their packs were consumed by abyssal beasts. Those who remain are fewer, but harder.




## **Alliances**

**Friendly With:** _The Steward Society_ (who study their prey reports for corruption spread).  
Uneasy alliance with _The Forge Guild_ — the Hounds often steal their supplies instead of requisitioning them properly.




## **Enemies**

**Aberrations, particularly “”**  
A parasitic hive-mind that sings through corrupted beasts. The Hounds have sworn eternal vengeance after the aberrations infested their kennels and killed hundreds of companions.




## **Effectiveness**

- **Complete objectives with consistency, but at high cost.**  
    When the Hounds hunt, their prey dies — even if the pack does too. Their tactics are suicidal, but their kill record is second only to the Iron Rain’s bombardments.
    



## **Major Achievements**

- **The Cleansing of the Bone Glades:** Hunted and eradicated a demonic contagion that turned trees into flesh.
    
- **The Seven-Day Pursuit:** Hunted the same fiend across three hundred miles of terrain without rest.
    
- **The Binding of Fangmare:** Captured and tamed a Rift-beast of immense size, now used as a living war engine.
    



## **Shameful Defeats**

- **The Feast of Chains:** An entire pack turned on each other after exposure to a demonic pheromone cloud. The survivors were executed by their own dogs.  
    To this day, the Hounds burn incense before battle to “mask their scent from temptation.”
