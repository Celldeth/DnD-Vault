# History


## Origins

Founded during a counter-offensive after the Battle of the Broken Ridge — when countless Rift Knights were slaughtered by overwhelming demon numbers. Survivors of that battle swore that no Knight would ever retreat again. They formed the Red Axe as a warband devoted to overwhelming retaliation — an order that breaks sieges, collapses lines, and turns terror back on the enemy.

## Achievements

The Red Dawn Offensive, where they broke a Rift incursion of 40,000 demons in a single day.

The Burning Line of Skalrend, a defensive stand so violent it turned an entire valley into a sea of molten glass.

## Defeats

The Fury’s End — when an entire company charged without waiting for support and vanished into a Riftstorm. Their screams echoed for days.

The Warbrand Revolt, when unblooded recruits rose against their commanders in blind rage, slaughtering half the fortress before order was restored.



# Culture



## Philosophy

The Red Axe lives by the creed of The Unending Charge — a doctrine that glorifies relentless motion and the total annihilation of the foe. They believe hesitation invites death, and rage is the truest purity a warrior can attain. To them, fury is not madness — it is divine focus, a burning clarity that burns away fear and doubt.

They are shock troops, destroyers, and berserk saints of the Rift Knights — unleashed when stalemate or despair must be shattered.

## Faith / Belief



## Demeanor

Loud, brash, and violently passionate. They laugh in battle and sing before charges. Every wound is a mark of pride, every death an offering to their cause. Though savage, they are deeply loyal - brothers and sisters forged in shared bloodlust.

## Order Flaw

They refuse to retreat or yield, regardless of circumstance. Tactical withdrawals are seen as dishonor; even regrouping is shameful. Their unwillingness to disengage often leads to catastrophic losses — but never surrender.

## Combat Doctrine

Shock and Awe / Close Quarters.
The Red Axe are mobile berserkers who excel at breaching enemy lines, wielding axes, blades, and heavy weapons in devastating melee. They specialize in overwhelming assaults, relentless aggression, and momentum-based warfare. Once the charge begins, nothing stops them.


# Organization

## Head Fortress

Skalrend, a shattered highland fortress carved into a volcanic cliff. The land is barren and scarred, the air thick with ash. Rivers of molten rock feed the forges where the Order’s weapons are made — each one quenched in blood and fire.

### Rule

Direct rule. The Red Axe lead through dominance and respect earned in combat. Skalrend’s citizens live under martial law, but all are trained to fight. Disputes are settled through ritualized combat — blood trials that rarely end in death, but always in pain.

### Region



## Mobility

They travel in a mobile fortress known as The Hearth of War — a massive armored land-cruiser powered by infernal engines salvaged from Rift wreckage. It roars across battlefields, leaving smoke and fire in its wake, serving as both fortress and forge.

## Training

Training instills unbreakable resolve; retreat is never an option.
Recruits are subjected to pain endurance trials, flame baptisms, and blood duels. They learn to channel rage into clarity — to fight through agony and exhaustion until nothing remains but will and muscle.

Advanced members are taught The Blood Trance: a ritual that lets them sustain combat for days, their pain numbed by sacred fury.

## Restricted

Rogues and Wizards rarely find a place here; subtlety and finesse are alien concepts.

Artificers are tolerated only if they forge weapons worth wielding.

## Equipment

Rageplate: Armor that channels adrenaline through arcane runes, granting bursts of speed and strength.

Bloodfire Axe: A weapon forged in magma and quenched in blood; ignites when it tastes demon flesh.

Warbrand Tattoos: Magical ink that glows crimson during battle, amplifying morale and ferocity.

## Order Strength

Overfilled with Knights.
The Red Axe attract every orphan, exile, and blood-drunk mercenary with something to prove. Their numbers swell easily — but discipline thins with each new zealot.
