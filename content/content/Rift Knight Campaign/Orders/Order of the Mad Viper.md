Your reflexes are honed by ritual venom exposure and years of battlefield cunning.
You gain advantage on initiative rolls, and you can apply poison to a weapon or piece of ammunition as a bonus action instead of an action.




# History


## Origins

A counter-offensive force created to hunt down and annihilate heretical cells and demonic infiltrators that escaped other Orders’ campaigns. Originally a clean-up corps, their lethal efficiency made them infamous.


## Achievements

Annihilated a demonic host in the Siege of Evershade with a three-hour, nonstop alchemical barrage.

Pioneered the creation of venom-forged bullets that dissolve through armor.

## Defeats

During the Glass Wastes Campaign, their unstable ammo detonated prematurely, erasing an entire battalion. They call it The Shedding — a necessary purge, according to their doctrine.



# Culture



## Philosophy

The Mad Vipers believe that speed, precision, and chaos win wars before they begin. They embody the venomous strike - hit hard, hit fast, and leave the enemy reeling before they can respond. To them, hesitation is a sin. Every member trains to become a living weapon - sharp, lethal, and adaptable.

## Demeanor

Fast-talking, overconfident, and slightly unhinged. They revel in risk, often taunting enemies mid-battle. Among other Orders, they are seen as unpredictable and dangerously self-reliant.


## Order Flaw

They hate doing anything that covers their livery. The green-black scales and serpent insignia must always be visible — even if it compromises stealth or protection. Some even etch the symbol onto their weapons or skin, believing it channels the serpent’s wrath.

## Combat Doctrine

Ranged and Shock & Awe.
Vipers strike fast with custom firearms and alchemically-enhanced bullets, then vanish before retaliation. They specialize in ambushes, rapid repositioning, and coordinated barrages that break morale before melee even begins.


# Organization

## Head Fortress

Tareth’s Maw, a jagged wasteland canyon riddled with caves and sinkholes. The wind howls like screaming serpents through its stone fangs. It’s rich in black sand and volatile crystal veins — both used in their alchemical ammunition.

### Rule

They exercise distant stewardship over Tareth’s Maw — preferring to let the people fend for themselves so long as trade flows and recruits are provided. Their presence is mostly symbolic: murals, banners, and rumors of the fanged watchmen in the cliffs.


## Mobility

They operate from a mobile fortress called The Coil — a segmented train of armored crawlers that snake across desert wastes. Each car is modular: some serve as barracks, others as ammo forges, and one as a shrine.

## Training

Fast, demoralizing strikes are drilled constantly, prioritizing speed, precision, and psychological dominance. Initiates practice marksmanship while sprinting or dodging, learn to reload blindfolded.

## Restricted

Clerics and Paladins are rare among them; faith is replaced by instinct.

Druids are forbidden — the Order distrusts naturalists and “beast-lovers.”

## Equipment

Modified Firearms with coiled serpent engraving — barrels infused with alchemical toxins.

Venom Rounds (poisoned ammunition that inflicts additional necrotic damage).

Serpent’s Bite Gauntlet: a retractable fang-blade used when foes get too close.


## Order Strength

Normal. Their numbers fluctuate — they replenish easily due to their reputation among thrill-seekers and misfits, but suffer heavy casualties due to their reckless doctrine.
