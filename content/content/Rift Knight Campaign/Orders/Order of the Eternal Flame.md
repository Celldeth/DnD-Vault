## Feature: Cleansing Presence


Your aura radiates holy heat. Once per day, you may perform a **Purification Rite** — a 10-minute ritual that removes disease, corruption, or mild curse effects from an ally or item. Alternatively, you can use it to ignite an area (10 ft radius) with sanctified flame that harms demons and undead but leaves humanoids untouched.



# History


## Origins

**Crusade.**  
When demonic corruption began spreading uncontrollably, a sect of the Cloth of the Burning declared a crusade to scour it from the Rift. The Eternal Flame became their militant arm — zealots wielding fire as both weapon and scripture.


## Major Achievements

**The Cleansing of Tarnished Depths:** An entire demonic hive burned out from underground with sanctified fire.

**The Siege of Tenfold Spire:** The victory that birthed their legend.

**Operation Scorched Halo:** Vaporized a demonic horde — and a city with it.

## Shameful Defeats

**The Burning of Solara’s Reach:** They mistook a refugee colony for a corrupted cult. Over 10,000 innocents burned.

**The Ash Oath Rebellion:** Several of their own ranks mutinied, claiming the Order had lost the Flame’s true purpose.



# Culture



## Philosophy

The Eternal Flame believes that corruption can only be purged through fire, literal or spiritual. They see flame as the perfect manifestation of divine will: unrelenting, purifying, and indiscriminate. To the Purifiers, to be cleansed by the flame is mercy. The weakness must be burned so only strength remains.

They preach that every soul carries a spark of divine flame, but that spark can be smothered by sin, demonic influence, or cowardice. To rekindle it, one must suffer, repent, and burn it away - metaphorically or otherwise.


## Faith / Belief

They worship [[Flame of Continuance]] — a divine manifestation of purity, judgment, and rebirth. 





## Demeanor

- Fanatical and uncompromising
- See fire as both punishment and blessing
- Obsessive about cleansing corruption in all forms
- Treat death by fire as martyrdom and honor
- using anything from the rift, even in benefit, is a point of corruption - therefore heresy.


## Order Flaw

The Purifiers’ zeal often turns **inward**. They see heresy everywhere — even among their allies. More than one Rift Knight regiment has been burned “for their own good.” Other Orders distrust them deeply, fearing they may one day turn their torches on the Union itself.


## Combat Doctrine

**Shock and Awe / Siege / Fire Magic.**  
The Eternal Flame devastates with alchemical fire, bombardments, and divine flame. Their tactics rely on overwhelming light, terror, and immolation.


# Organization

## Head Fortress

**Home:** Emberfast Citadel — carved into the caldera of an active volcano. Magma channels run through its sanctums, powering forges and baptismal pyres alike.  

**Rule:** _Direct._ The Order governs Emberfast with fiery law — heretics are burned, and dissent is met with “purification.”


## Mobility

The Order rarely travels in secrecy. They march beneath banners of smoke and flame, often aboard **the Pyrestorm Convoy** — mobile siege engines and incineration machines that double as shrines.

## Training

Purifiers undergo **fire baptism** — ritual scarification and endurance trials. They are drilled in **pyromancy**, **flamethrower warfare**, and **purification rites**. Many are both priests and soldiers, chanting hymns as they ignite the battlefield. Pain is part of prayer; suffering is strength.


## Restricted

The Order does not train Rogues or Rangers — stealth and subtlety are viewed as cowardice before divine truth. They prefer clerics, paladins, fighters, and warlocks devoted to fiery patrons.


## Equipment

- **Censer of Burning Faith:** A censer that emits divine flame and incense, bolstering allies’ resolve.
- **Pyrestorm Projector:** A modified flamethrower powered by volatile Rift-crystals.
- **Flamebrand Armor:** Plate etched with holy sigils that glow when the wearer calls upon divine fire.


## Troop Strength

**Normal to Overfilled.** Their fiery zeal attracts converts easily. However, many die in training — or in their own “purification” ceremonies.



## Recruitment

### The Rite of Ash and Flame

_The initiation trial of the Crimson Oath Paladins_


#### Step 1. The Gathering

- New initiates are brought to the The Ardent Tower, where the The Flame of Endurance burns.
- Veterans of the Sunforged Legion stand in a circle, armor of white and gold, chanting the _Litany of Ash_. Their voices are like a war-drum.


#### Step 2. The Confession

- Each initiate must confess their sins aloud before the chamber. They are told:  
    _“You enter not as who you were, but as ash, burned away. Speak, and be purged.”_
- Secrets can be real (encouraging roleplay) or invented (to show how much they’ll give up).
- Anything left unspoken is believed to “feed the fiends.”



#### Step 3. The Brand

- A heated brand, marked with the Ring of Flame, is pressed into their chest or palm. take 2d6 fire damage.
- If they refuse, they are cast into the flame. to give them “purpose in death.”


#### Step 4. The Chalice of Ash

- The initiate is given the Chalice: a cup mixed with blood, demon ichor, and sanctified oil.
- Drinking it causes searing pain as the divine oath burns through them.
- Effect: The drinker must succeed on a **Constitution save DC 15** or have one of the demon ichor effects warp them.


#### Step 5. The First Battle

- The trial ends with the Gate Test: initiates are lead on an ambush mission, already setup.
- They are given only shield and blade, told: _“Kill, or die.”_
- Surviving earns them their place in the Order.
- If one falls, the others are told: _“Then you will fight twice as hard, for their soul burns now in our stead.”_


#### Lasting Effects for PCs (if they take part)

- **Scar of the Oath (Mark of the Brand):** A faint crimson glow under the skin where branded. Fiends can smell it — granting advantage on Insight checks against fiends, but disadvantage on Deception checks against them.
    
- **Vision of the Rift:** Once per long rest, they can see through magical darkness for 1 minute (a lingering gift of the Chalice’s visions).
    
- **Psychological Weight:** PCs may feel the crushing burden of being part of something eternal. The Crimson Oath expects _forever_.
