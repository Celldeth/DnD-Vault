# General Information

The sky has 3 moons, a red moon that has a weekly cycle, a white moon that has a monthly cycle and a blue moon that has somewhat yearly cycle. The world also has 3 rings - violet, amber, and rose colored that are said to be rare magical metals that rarely fall to surface. It's sun is a white dwarf star called Tharmekhûl - after the dwarven god of fire and forge.



# Campaigns

Depending on the campaign setting, the type of game can change dramatically.


## Vampire Campaign

**Overview**: Dark Fantasy, renaissance technology, airships, high magic, outcast players, against the norm playstyle.


### Core Assumptions

- Gods oversee the world from the spirit realm (which is both the astral plane and the ethereal plane combined)

- Gods exert influence over the world through their followers

- Quasi-deities roam the material plane until they ascend to godhood

- Much of the world is untamed and wild regions abound

- Few know what lies beyond the mountains or in the depths of great forests of their city

- The world of Mal’gama is thought to be mostly mapped but not fully discovered nor explored

- Only a few civilizations of the past and their knowledge survive in legends, magic items, and their ruins.

- Conflict shapes the world’s history, powerful individuals strive to make their mark on the world

- The world is magical - including natural occurring flora and minerals containing magical properties, requiring expertise to utilize them.

- Magic users are relatively few in number, and often employed as personal mages to those in high power, and leave evidence of their craft everywhere.

  

### Setting the Stage

- You are on the continent of Elderon, in the Jaderun empire. The Jaderun Empire is referred as just "the empire" and the region that it controls is referred as Jaderun. The empire covers controls a large stretch of land, the many villages that the empire used to hold have merged and unified into a few larger cities, contributing to great social feats and obstacles. 

- You have been a vampire spawn for years, made by Lady Aranel, who belittled and berated you since being turned. She would often publicly humiliate you, being blamed for her own failures. She displays her wealth and power, such as extravagant feasts for her vampire guests and gaudy decorations in an attempt to hide the dismal condition of the castle. She would often order you to accompany her and expected you to praise her. Aranel would also constantly lash out on the slightest perceived slight. 

- Since becoming a vampire spawn, your memories have been fractured. Some remember more than others, perhaps remembering their hometown, loved ones. Perhaps you still hold relics from your past to help you remember your previous life. (This is not intended to hinder your backstory, this will only be flavor to "hand wave" things that are absent in your backstory, or for DM to improvise backstory arcs)

- Aranel's home is a gothic castle, Bothmer Manor, an old building slowly falling into ruin. It is located in the Hissing Thicket, west of Brightwick, the city that you were sent to steal trinkets and other errands.

- Brightwick has an airy architecture with white stone buildings and brightly painted doors and shutters. large windows and glass-paned rooftops.

- Through Aranel's feasts you've met a handful of vampires capable of tolerating her boastful parading through the castle. Lord Hailar Frenzel, Lady Zargava Dorn, Count Vladan Menek, Baroness Kridira Schrade, Sir Osip Warhorn, and Duchess Mira Hahnel. these vampires are spread throughout the empire and beyond, perhaps they could be persuaded to offer guidance on how to grow your vampiric powers.
