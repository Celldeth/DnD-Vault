## Feature: Silent Step

You’ve mastered the art of moving unseen and unheard.
You can attempt to hide even when only lightly obscured, and you leave behind no scent or trace unless you choose to. Additionally, once per long rest, you can move at full speed while hidden without disadvantage on Stealth checks for 1 minute.





# History


## Origins

Created as a supportive division for reconnaissance and surgical elimination of enemy leadership. They were originally scouts and spies within the Rift Knight legions, formalized into an Order after their covert work ended several prolonged wars with minimal loss of life.

## Achievements

The Silent Purge of Ravenholdt — the extermination of an entire cult before their first summoning.

Recovered the Mirror of Echoes from the Shadow Rift without alerting its aberrant guardians.

## Defeats

The Whisper War — when internal schism caused a faction of Daggers to turn on each other, breaking the sacred silence and exposing their network to the enemy. The survivors swore never to speak again.



# Culture



## Philosophy

The Shadowed Daggers believe that a single, unseen strike is worth more than a thousand swords in open war.
They embody silence, precision, and information warfare - infiltrators, assassins, and saboteurs operating deep within enemy lines. Their philosophy is built on the doctrine of “Quiet Death”: to kill without being known, to kill without being seen, and to vanish before the scream fades.

## Faith / Belief



## Demeanor

Cold, focused, and eerily calm. They rarely speak, even off duty, and their presence often unnerves allies. To outsiders, they seem emotionless — but among themselves, they share a quiet fraternity, communicating through gestures, code, and ritual hand signs.

## Order Flaw

They refuse to speak in common language when in uniform or on duty. Instead, they use hand signals, coded whispers, or telepathic links (if available). Other Orders find this unnerving and suspect it hides treachery — but for the Daggers, it’s discipline and respect for the silence that defines them.

## Combat Doctrine

Stealth and Close Quarters.
Every Dagger learns to move like shadow and kill in silence. They rely on ambush, distraction, and quick lethal strikes — ending fights before the first alarm sounds. When stealth fails, they vanish and strike again elsewhere.


# Organization

## Head Fortress

Duskvale, a hidden mountain valley perpetually cloaked in mist and shadow. The terrain is sheer cliffs, winding passes, and obsidian caves. Locals are known for their quiet nature and unnatural night vision, rumored to descend from those the Daggers trained long ago.

### Rule

They practice distant stewardship, governing only through covert means — spies, informants, and quiet enforcers. Most of Duskvale’s citizens have never seen a Knight of the Dagger, but they all obey the laws they whisper into being.


## Mobility

They maintain a chain of light wagons, each built to blend into terrain and break down into portable shelters. When moving, the convoy resembles a caravan of wandering merchants, musicians, or monks - never what it truly is.

## Training

Specialists in silent movement, infiltration, and misdirection.
Novitiates are trained from childhood in body control, breathing discipline, and sensory awareness. Advanced members learn shadow manipulation, psychic focus, and magical concealment techniques — merging martial skill with arcane stealth.

## Restricted

Barbarians and Paladins are unheard of among them — too loud, too rigid.

Open casters (Sorcerers, Bards) are discouraged unless their magic aids concealment.

## Equipment

Dagger of the Veil: a curved shortblade enchanted to muffle nearby sound when drawn.

Shadowmantle Cloak: renders the wearer lightly obscured in dim light or darkness.

Codex of Silence: a ritual text containing the Order’s cipher and meditative litanies.

## Order Strength

Below Average. Their ranks are thin, for training a Dagger takes decades — and most die on missions no one will ever know occurred.

## Alliances

The Arcanum, who value their intelligence-gathering and discreet handling of magical threats.

## Enemies / Rivals

Aberrations, whose chaotic minds disrupt stealth and mental focus.

Demons of Whispering Madness, who weaponize noise and paranoia.

The Order of the Mad Viper, for their reckless, flamboyant warfare that undoes the Daggers’ careful groundwork.


## Reputation

Above-average performance.
They achieve results unseen by most, often preventing wars or assassinating key threats before others even realize danger was near. Few outside the Council know the full extent of their influence.
